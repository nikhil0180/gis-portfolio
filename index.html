Bridging Spatial Analysis, Cartography & Urban Decisions

I am a GIS Professional with a Master of Science in Geographic Information Science and Cartography from the University at Albany, SUNY, backed by over 2 years of combined experience across spatial analytics, infrastructure mapping, urban planning, and geospatial data management.

My work spans complex spatial statistics, pedestrian accessibility and network modeling, and remote sensing.
